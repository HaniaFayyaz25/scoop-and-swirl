🏠 Our story

Started in a small kitchen, grown with love

Scoop & Swirl began in 2023 in a two-room kitchen in DHA Phase 4 — one secondhand churner, Ammi’s kulfi recipe, and a freezer that rattled all night. We shared cups with neighbours, then their cousins, then the whole street.

Three years on, family and friends still taste-test every new flavour. We still churn every morning. The freezer is bigger. The love is the same.
